# Nexus product

The authoritative product document is `docs/PRD/structure.md`. Requirements
are identified by `PRD-xxx` IDs, and each one traces to an intake ID
(`R-*` or `D-*`). Every spec requirement must cite the `PRD-xxx` it
implements.
