- **Finish review.** Each review ran in a fresh Kiro sub-agent. Kiro ships
  no dedicated Impeccable reviewer, so the sub-agents followed Impeccable's
  reviewer definition.
  - The first review returned **fix** with eight items:
    - status colours;
    - the review bar covering the calendar;
    - a truncated linked issue;
    - activity times drifting from their entries;
    - disabled buttons after the review;

    - a scroll jump;
    - a text "!" used as an icon.
  - The first verdict pass scored six of those resolved and two partial, and
    found two regressions.
  - The second verdict pass scored all five open items resolved and found
    two small regressions: In progress icon contrast of 2.8:1, and the
    narrow board scrolled flush. Kiro fixed both with the reviewer's values

    reviewer has scored those last two fixes.
  - `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` were written afterwards by a
    separate documenter sub-agent from the built CSS. Will has not
    confirmed its "Decision Desk" north star or its colour names.
- **Defect found and repaired during the run.** A PowerShell edit passed a
  single text pair through a nested array. PowerShell flattened the array,
  so the script replaced single characters. It turned every `<` in
  `wireframe/index.html` into `d`.
  - Kiro restored the tags by pattern (901 tags, then 9 comments).
  - It then checked the DOM: 9 views, 24 icon symbols, 5 notes and no stray
    markup text. It re-rendered every view and reran the smoke test.
  - Lesson: edit files with `str_replace`, or one literal pair at a time,
    never with an array of pairs in PowerShell.
