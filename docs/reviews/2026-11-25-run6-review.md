## Other observations (no finding ID)

- **Quick switch.** Outside Settings, the switch does not say that pressing it leaves
  Match system. The reviewer suggests a short accessible description or a brief
  confirmation.
- **Backlink speed.** With 250 fictional notes and four links each, one backlinks request
  read every note and returned 1,000 rows in about 0.39 to 0.58 s, before HTTP and
  rendering. Tolerable for occasional navigation; a cache or index by source revision is
  suggested if that size is representative.
- **Screen readers.** The reviewer verified keyboard selection, insertion and Undo in Edge
  and Electron, but ran no NVDA or Narrator check. What a screen reader says for the
  selected option, including its folder, is unverified. The fallback insertion's Undo is
  also unverified.
- **Electron close with an unsaved draft.** In the reviewer's Electron completion test,
  closing with the draft unsaved timed out after 90 s; with an explicit Save it passed.
  The reviewer did not establish a cause. **Kiro's reading of the source (not verified):**
  the note page cancels `beforeunload` while a draft is unsaved
  (`apps/web/src/pages/NotesPage.tsx:220`), and Electron main has no
  `will-prevent-unload` handler, so the window may refuse to close.
- **The reviewer's judgements:**
  - The `<html>` attributes in the unauthenticated shell reveal only the mode and two
    palettes, from fixed enums; no exploit was found.
  - Electron reading the preference before the service answers is reasonable, once it
    validates as the service does (R6-11).
  - The approved light look is recognisable at 1360 × 900; the new switch is the
    noticeable change.
  - Keeping the bar on the rejected change until Send or Skip is appropriate.
  - Keeping only the reason for new issues, links and demo changes matches the agreed
    scope.
  - The correction's reason does not change the tools or grant acceptance. The write
    boundary must hold regardless of how a model reads it (R6-4, R6-5).
