## Dates

- The submission period ends **4 December 2026 at 2:00 pm Pacific Time
  (22:00 GMT)**. No extensions, and late entries are refused.
- Kiro credit requests (a one-time 4,000 credits per entrant) close on
  **1 December 2026 at 12:00 pm Pacific Time**, and codes must be redeemed by
  4 December.
- Judging runs from 16 December 2026 to 29 January 2027. Winners are
  announced on or around 3 February 2027.

## Eligibility and team

- Teams can have up to three eligible individuals. One Representative submits
  for the team.
- The project must be newly created during the submission period, which
  started on 30 October 2026. Frameworks, libraries and starter templates are
  allowed, but any other pre-existing code or work must be disclosed. The
  Nexus history before this work is a single one-line `README.md`, added on
  13 November and deleted on 24 November.

## Project requirements

- A working application built with Kiro (IDE, CLI, Web or Crew), entered in a
  category. Nexus is entered in **Frankenstein**: technologies that seem
  incompatible, stitched into one app. Judges may disqualify a project for
  insufficient use of Kiro.
- The app must install and run consistently on its intended platform, and
  behave as shown in the video and described in the text.
- Third-party APIs, SDKs and data must be used within their terms.

## Submission requirements

- A URL to the working application. Any login details and instructions go in
  the submission form.
- A text description of the features and functionality.
- A demo video under three minutes, public on YouTube, showing the project
  running on its intended device. It must not contain third-party trademarks
  or copyrighted music without permission.
- A public **GitHub** repository containing all source code, assets and
  instructions, and an open-source licence that GitHub detects in the
  repository's About section.
- A `/.kiro` directory at the repository root, showing specs, hooks and
  steering. It must **not** be listed in `.gitignore`, because that can
  disqualify the entry.
- The category and any bonus categories entered.
- A write-up of how Kiro was used. The prompts cover IDE, CLI, Web and Crew,
  custom agents, hooks, spec-driven development compared with vibe coding,
  steering, MCP, property-based testing and powers.
- Testing access for judges, free and without restriction until judging ends.
  Judges may score from the description and video alone.
- All materials in English.

## Judging

- Stage one is pass or fail: does the project fit the theme and make
  reasonable use of Kiro?
- Stage two weights five criteria equally, at 10 points each:
  - Potential Impact
  - Technological Implementation (depth and variety of Kiro use)
  - Design (a complete, polished product)
  - Innovation
  - Presentation (the video, end to end)

## Bonus prizes to consider (optional)

- Best Student Project: every team member must be a student.
- Social Blitz: a public social post that starts with `#KiroweenHackathon26`.
- Blog: a public post that mentions Kiroween 2026, published during the
  submission period.
- Best Demo: awarded on the Presentation score.

Whether to enter any bonus category is Will and Hein's decision.
